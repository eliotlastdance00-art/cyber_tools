Honest answers

No fine print, no fake reviews.

Is my document uploaded anywhere?

No. VoiceReader is a fully client-side web app. Text extraction (PDF.js) and speech synthesis both happen inside your browser. Nothing is transmitted to any server.

What does the free tier include?

Everything except length: you can read up to 1,500 characters per document, which is enough to evaluate the voices and the highlighting. Pro removes the limit and unlocks the document library.

Why a one-time payment instead of a subscription?

Because the app has no per-user server costs — voices come from your own device. We'd rather sell you a license once than rent you your own headphones.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email within 30 days of purchase and you get a full refund. No form, no questions.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

No — scanned PDFs are images, and VoiceReader deliberately avoids uploading your files to an OCR server. Run any OCR tool (e.g. the one built into your OS) first, then drop the result in.

Which languages are supported?

Any language your operating system has a speech voice for — typically English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Hindi and more. Voice quality depends on your system, not on us.
